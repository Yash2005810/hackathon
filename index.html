FAQs

Got questions? We've got answers!

Who can participate in HackVerse 2025? ▲
HackVerse 2025 is open to all college and university students from any discipline. Whether you're studying computer science, design, business, or any other field, you're welcome to participate!
Do I need a team to participate? ▼
You can participate solo or as a team of up to 4 members. We encourage teams as collaboration often leads to better solutions, but solo participants are absolutely welcome!
What should I bring to the hackathon? ▼
Bring your laptop, charger, any hardware you need for your project, and your enthusiasm! We'll provide food, beverages, and workspace. Feel free to bring sleeping bags if you plan to work through the night.
Is there any participation fee? ▼
No! HackVerse 2025 is completely free to participate. We provide meals, snacks, swag, and all necessary resources at no cost to participants.
What kind of projects can I build? ▼
You can build any project related to AR/VR or AI/ML. This includes mobile apps, web apps, hardware projects, games, or any creative solution that leverages these technologies.
Will there be mentors available? ▼
Yes! We have industry experts from top tech companies who will be available throughout the hackathon for one-on-one mentorship sessions.
What are the prizes? ▼
Total prize pool is $50,000+. First place wins $20,000, second place wins $10,000, and third place wins $5,000. Track-specific prizes and special category awards are also available.
Do I need prior experience in AR/VR or AI/ML? ▼
No prior experience required! We welcome beginners and experts alike. Our workshops and mentors will help you get up to speed on the technologies you need.
